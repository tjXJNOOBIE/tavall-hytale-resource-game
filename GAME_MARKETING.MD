# Word-of-Mouth

Our world and its story is manufactured by the players; we just give them the tools to create their own stories.

System count or their complexity alone doesn't determine if players will engage, we need to focus on what players will talk about to fuel  word-of-mouth.

* The types of questions we should be asking for this game is what players will actually talk about.

```text
Not:
“The companion system supports aging, ownership, and training metadata.”
But:
“My wolf grew up from a puppy, got injured defending a trade cart, and now our guild is raising resources to heal it.”
Not:
“There is a scouting system with confidence and stale intel.”
But:
“We got baited by fake scout reports and marched into an ambush.”
Not:
“There is a kingdom tax system.”
But:
“Our king raised taxes, so half the kingdom joined a rebellion and burned his storage hall.”
Not:
“There are NPCs.”
But:
 “The NPC blacksmith remembers who supplies iron, and our rival kingdom cut off the route.”
```
